The Problem

Reframing the Challenge

Old Framing

"Lack of a streamlined system for reporting injured cows."

Reframed as a Product Designer

High Response Latency & Information Asymmetry

A marketplace gap where demand (citizens witnessing injured animals) cannot efficiently reach supply (NGO ambulances) due to a lack of real-time geospatial data and transparent feedback loops.

⚡

Reporting

Lack of a streamlined system for citizens to report injured or stray cows in real-time.

👁

Visibility

Limited visibility and recognition beyond the existing local donor base.

🔍

Transparency

Donors have low transparency about how their funds are specifically utilized.

🤝

Engagement

Limited digital engagement with the youth and a new generation of potential volunteers.

Empathize

What We Learned

Primary research across citizens, donors, and volunteer networks revealed three core behavioral insights.

🔎

Citizens Want to Help

Primary research showed people want to help but lack an easy, instant reporting mechanism.

🛡

Donors Want Trust

Donors desire transparency, proof of impact, and a stronger emotional connection to their contributions.

📱

Need for Recognition

A modern, transparent digital interface is required to grow donations and recognition.

🧠

Behavioral Insight

Our research revealed that donors desire a stronger emotional connection to their contributions — not just a receipt, but visible proof of impact. This insight from Behavioral Psychology directly shaped our transparency and progress-tracking features, leveraging the Endowed Progress Effect to sustain donor engagement.

🔬

Technical Implementation

A fine-tuned image classification model (built on TensorFlow) trained on veterinary injury datasets. Categories include: fractures, lacerations, dehydration, infections, and behavioral distress. Confidence scores above 85% trigger automatic priority escalation.

📊

Impact on Response

By automating severity assessment, we reduce the manual triage step from ~3 minutes to under 5 seconds. Critical cases reach ambulance teams 4x faster, directly addressing the "High Response Latency" problem identified in our research.

Behavioral Psychology

Designing for Human Behavior

Applying behavioral science principles to drive sustained engagement — not just downloads, but lasting impact.

The Endowed Progress Effect shows that people are more motivated to complete a goal when they feel they've already made progress. By showing donors a live recovery timeline for "their" cow, we transform a one-time donation into an ongoing emotional commitment.

🏆

Volunteer Badges

Variable Reward Schedule
🦸 First Rescue Report your first SOS
⚡ Speed Hero Report under 30 sec
🌟 Community Star 5 rescues in a month
💚 Guardian Angel Adopted a cow
🔒 ??? Hidden badge
🔒 ??? Keep going!

Variable Reward Schedules (the same psychology behind social media engagement) keep volunteers coming back. Unlockable and hidden badges create anticipation. The "locked" badges trigger curiosity and drive repeated engagement, far more effectively than a simple "thank you" screen.
