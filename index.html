What We Learned

Research with citizens, donors, and volunteers revealed three clear needs.

🔎

Citizens Want to Help

People want to help, but reporting is not instant.

🛡

Donors Want Trust

Donors want proof of impact and a stronger connection.

📱

Need for Recognition

A modern interface can grow donations and recognition.

🧠

Behavioral Insight

Donors stay engaged when they can see visible progress. That shaped the recovery tracker and impact updates.

The Core Question

"How might we create a digital platform that...

  1. 01 Enables citizens to report injured cows instantly?
  2. 02 Allows donors to see the real-time impact of their contribution?
  3. 03 Builds a transparent, trustworthy, and engaging experience?
Ideate

The Gaurdian App — Core Features

Four focused features connect citizens, donors, volunteers, and the NGO.

🚨

SOS Cow Rescue: REPORT

One-tap report button with GPS location and photo upload to alert the ambulance team instantly.

💰

Donation Transparency

Choose categories (food, medicine) and get real-time updates, digital receipts, and impact summaries.

👥

Volunteer Network

Signup and activity tracker for local volunteers with rewards and recognition badges.

📢

Community Section

"Adopt a Cow" feature for personal sponsorship, plus educational stories and videos from the Gaushala.

AI Developer Edge

AI-Powered Injury Triage

A photo becomes a severity score, helping rescue teams prioritize the most critical cases.

📸

→

🚑

Smart Dispatch

🔬

Technical Implementation

A TensorFlow image model classifies injury type and severity from a citizen's photo. High-confidence cases are escalated automatically.

📊

Impact on Response

Automated triage reduces assessment from ~3 minutes to under 5 seconds, helping critical cases reach teams faster.
